About

I am Vishnu Iyengar. I grew up in India and came to New Jersey at fourteen, and I have spent most of my life since then between two worlds. I studied Industrial and Systems Engineering at Rutgers, and later earned an MBA and a Master of Business and Science in Computer and Information Sciences there.

My work sits where three things meet: Sanskrit, Carnatic music and machine learning. I work on the evaluation of AI systems and on data science, and I spend the rest of my time with Sanskrit texts and with music. The question that ties my writing together is what happens to Indian ideas when they are carried into English, whether by translators or by machines.

Finsnovai is my studio. It builds learning tools for Sanskrit, starting with a daily vibhakti practice site, and it is where I publish my essays and research.

The studio

Our first tool is a daily practice site for vibhakti, the case endings of Sanskrit nouns. A session takes about five minutes: twelve questions and two declension tables. The site keeps track of the endings each learner forgets and brings those back first.

It works on phones and computers, works offline after the first visit, and needs no account. It is free.
