Learning tools for Sanskrit

Five minutes a day, until the endings come without thinking.

Finsnovai is a small studio building learning software and videos rooted in Sanskrit. Our first product is a daily vibhakti practice site for students who want noun endings to become second nature.

Vibhakti daily practice

A short daily session of twelve questions and two declension tables. It remembers which endings you are forgetting and brings those back first.

  • Runs on any phone or computer
  • Works without internet once opened
  • No account, no tracking, free to use
  • Everything you read and answer is in Devanagari
